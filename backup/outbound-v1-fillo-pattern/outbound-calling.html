Telza Outbound Calling

Stop dialing. Start conversations.

Hours of manual dialing, missed follow-ups and leads that go cold before anyone calls back. Telza takes the calling off your team's plate: it places personalised calls in parallel, talks with each person naturally and hands your team the conversations, and the outcomes, that matter.

How outbound AI calling works

Inside one outbound call

  1. 01

    The campaign starts

    Your contact list, goals and script load, and calls only go out inside the calling windows you set.

  2. 02

    Telza dials

    The call goes out. If voicemail answers, Telza leaves a tailored message and schedules a retry.

  3. 03

    A personal conversation

    The agent uses details from your CRM and follows your campaign logic, so the call sounds personal, not scripted.

  4. 04

    Questions and objections

    Common objections are handled and the person is qualified against your criteria.

  5. 05

    Outcome logged

    The appointment is confirmed or the conversation is routed to your team, and the outcome is written back to your CRM.
