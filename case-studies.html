An AI Receptionist for a Busy Outpatient Clinic

The scenario

A busy outpatient clinic with several practitioners takes most of its bookings by phone. The same reception team answers calls, checks patients in and manages the schedule, often all at once.

The challenge

  • Calls ring out during the morning and lunchtime rush, and some patients don’t call back.
  • Evening and weekend booking requests go to voicemail and wait until the next working day.
  • Reminder calls are hard to fit into a busy day, so forgotten appointments leave gaps in the schedule.
  • Post-visit follow-ups depend on someone remembering to make them.

The Telza solution

  • An inbound AI voice agent answers every call as a 24/7 receptionist and books, moves or cancels appointments from the clinic’s real availability.
  • WhatsApp reminders go out before each visit, and patients can confirm or reschedule in the same chat.
  • Routine questions about opening hours, directions and visit preparation are answered straight away. Clinical questions are passed to staff with a summary of the call.
  • Outbound follow-up calls check in after visits and book the next appointment when one is needed.

How it is set up

  • Map the front desk: services, booking rules, opening hours and the questions patients ask most.
  • Connect and configure: link the agent to the scheduling system and decide which calls go to staff.
  • Start small, then expand: begin with overflow and after-hours calls, review the conversations, then extend to all calls.

What the clinic could expect

  • Patients reach someone every time they call, day or night.
  • Reception staff spend more time with the patients in front of them.
  • Fewer forgotten appointments thanks to reminders and easy rescheduling.
  • Consistent follow-up after every visit.

E-commerce · Illustrative case study

24/7 Order and Product Support for an Online Fashion Store

The scenario

An online fashion store sells across its website and social channels. A small support team handles email, chat and social messages, and volumes jump during sales and new collection launches.

The challenge

  • “Where is my order?” questions fill the inbox and push more complex requests down the queue.
  • Shoppers with sizing, stock or delivery questions leave the site when nobody answers quickly.
  • Returns and exchanges need several back-and-forth messages before anything happens.
  • Peak periods bring more messages than the team can answer the same day.

The Telza solution

  • An e-commerce AI chatbot on the website answers order status, delivery and returns questions instantly from live order data.
  • The same assistant helps shoppers with sizing, materials and stock, and suggests products that match what they are looking for.
  • WhatsApp automation sends order confirmations, shipping updates and cart reminders, and customers can reply for help in the same chat.
  • Complex cases are handed to the support team with the full conversation, so nobody has to repeat themselves.

How it is set up

  • Gather the knowledge: product catalogue, store policies and the questions customers ask most.
  • Connect the store: link order and product data, and set rules for when to involve the team.
  • Launch and refine: go live on the website and WhatsApp, review conversations and improve answers over time.

What the store could expect

  • Instant answers for shoppers at any hour, including during sales.
  • A lighter support inbox, with the team focused on cases that need a person.
  • Help at the moment of purchase, when shoppers are deciding.
  • Customers kept informed about their orders without having to ask.

This is an illustrative case study based on common e-commerce workflows. It does not describe a specific client, and no performance figures are claimed.

Logistics · Illustrative case study

Proactive Delivery Updates for a Regional Courier

The scenario

A regional courier delivers parcels for retailers and local businesses. Its customer service team answers calls from senders and recipients while also coordinating with drivers.

The challenge

  • “Where is my parcel?” calls take up much of the team’s day and spike whenever there is a delay.
  • Recipients aren’t always told when a delivery is coming, so some deliveries fail and have to be repeated.
  • Changing a delivery day or pickup time means a phone call or an email thread.

The Telza solution

  • An inbound AI voice agent answers tracking calls at any time and gives the current status and delivery window.
  • WhatsApp updates are sent at each stage, from dispatch to out for delivery to delivered.
  • Recipients can reschedule a delivery or book a pickup in the same call or chat, without waiting for an agent.
  • Automated calls and messages warn customers about delays and follow up after a missed delivery.

How it is set up

  • Map the journey: delivery stages, service areas, rescheduling rules and common customer questions.
  • Connect the systems: link the agents to tracking and dispatch data so answers and updates are always current.
  • Roll out by channel: start with tracking calls, add proactive WhatsApp updates, then rescheduling.

What the courier could expect

  • Customers informed before they need to ask.
  • Fewer routine tracking calls reaching the team.
  • Fewer failed deliveries, because recipients can easily choose a time that works.
  • A team focused on operations and exceptions instead of status updates.

This is an illustrative case study based on common logistics workflows. It does not describe a specific client, and no performance figures are claimed.
