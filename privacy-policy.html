Privacy Policy

How Telza collects, uses and protects your information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Introduction

Telza (“Telza”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides AI voice agents, WhatsApp automation, AI chatbots and custom AI automation for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have when you visit our website or use our services.

When our business customers use Telza to talk to their own customers, for example through an AI voice agent or a WhatsApp conversation, we process that information on their behalf and according to their instructions. In that case, the business you are speaking with is responsible for how your information is used, and you should also read its privacy policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details, such as your name, work email address, phone number and company name, when you book a demo, subscribe to updates or contact us.
  • Account information, such as login details and settings, when you use our services.
  • Messages and requests you send to our team, including support requests.

Information processed through our services

  • Conversation data, such as call recordings, transcripts, chat messages and the details collected during a conversation, when a business uses Telza to communicate with its customers.
  • Business data that our customers connect to Telza, such as calendar availability, CRM records or order information, so their AI agents can answer and act correctly.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical and usage information, such as your browser type, device, IP address, the pages you visit and how you interact with our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • provide, operate and maintain our services, including running AI agents and workflows for our customers;
  • respond to demo requests, questions and support requests;
  • send service updates and, where you have agreed, product news, which you can unsubscribe from at any time;
  • improve, secure and troubleshoot our website and services;
  • meet our legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Protection

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. These include access controls, encryption in transit where supported and limiting access to people who need it to do their work.

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law or our agreements with customers. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so while we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, such as local storage, to keep the site working properly, remember your preferences and understand how visitors use it. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

6. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers to deliver our website and services. Depending on the service, these may include:

  • hosting and cloud infrastructure providers;
  • telephony and messaging providers, such as WhatsApp, used to place calls and send messages;
  • AI and speech technology providers that power our agents;
  • tools that our customers choose to connect, such as their CRM, calendar or e-commerce platform;
  • website resources, such as Google Fonts and content delivery networks, which may receive your IP address when our pages load.

These providers may only use your information to provide their services to us or our customers and are subject to their own privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, or if you would like to make a request about your personal information, please contact us through our Support page.
